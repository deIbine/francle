Guess the hidden French region in 6 guesses or fewer.

Daily game: the same region for everyone, one game per day,
always among the current regions. Free game: as many as you like,
with the regions chosen in the Settings menu (former or current).
Only daily games count in the statistics (Game menu).

Département bonus: after winning the daily game, find each département of the region
on the map (one click per département; green: found, red: missed).
For a region with a single département, give its number. The bonus counts from the first
answer, even if skipped or interrupted, and its average score appears in the statistics.

After each guess, the table shows:
  • the distance between the capital of your guess and that of the hidden region;
  • the direction to follow to reach the hidden region (↑ north, → east…);
  • the proximity percentage (100%: you are there).

Each wrong guess reveals a clue: the area, a food specialty,
a famous person, the regional capital, then a landmark.
Guessing the same region twice does not cost a guess.

Accents, capitals, spaces and hyphens are ignored: “ile de france” is accepted,
and so are the French names (“Bretagne”).
While you type, suggestions appear: ↓ and ↑ to pick one, Enter to take it,
then Enter again to guess (they can be turned off in the Settings menu).

The Settings menu also offers a dark theme, distances in miles,
a randomly rotated silhouette to make the game harder, and the language (French or English).
They are remembered from one launch to the next.

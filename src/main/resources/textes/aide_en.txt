Guess the hidden French region in 6 guesses or fewer.

After each guess, the table shows:
  • the distance between the capital of your guess and that of the hidden region;
  • the direction to follow to reach the hidden region (↑ north, → east…);
  • the proximity percentage (100%: you are there).

Each wrong guess reveals a clue: the area, a food specialty,
a famous person, the regional capital, then a landmark. Once the game is
over, all the clues are shown.
Guessing the same region twice does not cost a guess.

Daily game: the same region for everyone, one game per day,
always among the current regions; it changes at midnight. Free game: as many as
you like, with the regions chosen in the Settings menu (former or current).
Only daily games count in the statistics (Game menu), where you can also
copy your result to share it, without giving the answer away.

Département bonus 🗺️: after each win, click on each département of the region, then
type its name or its number (with the keyboard: Tab to the map, the arrow keys, then
Enter). One answer per département (green: found, red: missed), and your score is
added to your shared result. For a region with a single département, give its number.
The daily bonus counts from the first answer, even if skipped or interrupted,
and its average score appears in the statistics.

Accents, capitals, spaces and hyphens are ignored: “ile de france” is accepted,
and so are the French names (“Bretagne”). While you type, suggestions appear:
↓ and ↑ to pick one, Enter to take it, then Enter again to guess.
Enter on the start of a name (“brit”) plays the only region it matches.
The list of regions (Game menu) also lets you guess a region with one click.

The Settings menu offers a dark theme, distances in miles, the language,
and two hard modes: a rotated 🌀 or hidden 🙈 silhouette. They are set at
the first guess: in a game already started, a change counts for the next one.
